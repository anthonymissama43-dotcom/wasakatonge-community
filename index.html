01 WHO WE ARE

Different people. One direction.

You don't have to fit in. The community finds where you fit.

Wasakatonge exists for people who want to move beyond talking about possibilities and start creating them.

We bring different strengths into one environment: skills, knowledge, capital, ideas, networks, experience and determination.

Nobody has to have everything. The strength is in what we can build together.

OUR VISION

A community where skills meet opportunities.

To build a community where different skills, resources and ideas come together to create opportunities, businesses, projects and sustainable growth.
